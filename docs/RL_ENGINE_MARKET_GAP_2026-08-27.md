# Rocket League engine market gap — 2026-08-27

This is a capability-positioning audit, not proof that no competitor has an
unpublished equivalent. Public claims must remain narrower than the evidence.

## What current products publicly emphasize

- [Replay Bench](https://www.replaybench.com/) publicly emphasizes per-frame
  Threat scoring, possession breakdowns, a 3D viewer and coaching notes.
- [ReplayLabs](https://replaylabs.app/) publicly emphasizes detailed grading,
  timelines, advanced stats, a 2D viewer and replay/group APIs.
- [trophi.ai](https://trophiai.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RL2/pages/901480449/Rocket%2BLeague%2BFAQ)
  publicly emphasizes automatic replay analysis, training paths, voice coaching
  and trend overlays across the last game and last ten games.
- [RL Coach](https://rlcoach.org/) publicly emphasizes replay parsing, AI
  coaching, mistake reports, mechanic guides and short training plans.

## Defensible Replay Method wedge

The differentiated target is not “more AI notes” or another replay grade. It is
an auditable behavior-improvement loop with all of these properties together:

1. every comparable decision is counted as firing, non-firing or abstained;
2. detector, opportunity, context and threshold versions are frozen;
3. recurrence is opportunity-normalized across matches instead of counting raw
   findings;
4. baseline and follow-up compare only like-for-like contexts;
5. the engine can return improvement, regression, resolution or inconclusive;
6. human-review moments and label provenance remain auditable;
7. public output remains gated when evidence is insufficient.

The reviewed competitor pages describe replay grades, threat, stats, AI notes,
training and trend displays. They do not publicly document this complete
version-locked opportunity-denominator plus intervention-verification contract.
That makes it a credible differentiation hypothesis, not an absolute exclusivity
claim.

## Claim boundary

Safe: “Replay Method is being built to measure the same versioned decision
pattern before and after practice, with explicit abstention and replay evidence.”

Unsafe today: “No competitor can do this”, “the most accurate Rocket League
coach”, or any superiority claim without a reproducible head-to-head benchmark.
