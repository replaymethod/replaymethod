# Growth and monetization backlog

Last consolidated: 2026-08-18

This document preserves owner input for the post-engine commercial work. Ideas
are hypotheses until measured; implementation must remain truthful, compliant
and consistent with the evidence-first product promise.

## Immediate product hypotheses

1. Reduce time-to-value: place the supported replay drop zone on the landing
   page and let a visitor begin before creating an account or giving an email.
2. Ask for email after the first useful result, when saving the report or
   starting the improvement loop has clear value.
3. Add a short visual "how it works" path beside the upload control, with input
   requirements, privacy, processing state and the expected report outcome.
4. Make the interface feel responsive and game-native through purposeful
   progress, evidence playback and clear actions—not distracting motion or
   dark-pattern stimulation.
5. Review the complete mobile funnel for minimum reading, minimum clicks,
   accessible controls and fast perceived feedback.
6. Add a real checkout only after entitlements, usage limits, cancellation,
   receipts, privacy, support and successful-report measurement work end to end.

## Pricing research hypotheses

- Preserve a useful limited free entry point and measure conversion after a
  successful analysis.
- Compare monthly, three-month, six-month and annual commitments against real
  competitors, processing cost and retention data before choosing prices.
- Test annual-first presentation only as a transparent value comparison. Show
  total charge, effective monthly price, renewal and cancellation clearly.
- Candidate numbers such as $15 monthly, $25/3 months, $45/6 months and $70/year
  are unvalidated inputs, not approved prices.
- Never guarantee rank improvement or use refund wording with hidden escape
  clauses. Any guarantee must have plain, fair, measurable conditions and a
  modeled loss ceiling.

## Owner actions: distribution and community

- Create a consistent brand presence on Discord, TikTok, Instagram and X with a
  credible initial content library combining useful analysis, educational clips
  and transparent product demonstrations.
- Evaluate Google Search, TikTok and Discord placements through small capped
  tests measured by successful report and retained paid customer—not clicks.
- Participate in relevant Reddit and community discussions as Replay Method or
  with clear affiliation, contribute useful analysis first, and follow each
  community's self-promotion rules.
- Do not impersonate independent customers, manufacture testimonials, spam
  communities or mass-follow scraped audiences. These tactics create platform,
  consumer-protection and brand risk.

The owner operates these channels and creates/publishes the content. Codex owns
the supporting product work: conversion flow, analytics events, landing-page
experiments, entitlement/payment infrastructure and measured technical results.

## Research and implementation order

1. Finish the reliable Rocket League analysis and owner review workflow.
2. Instrument upload start, successful report, usefulness, save/email intent,
   upgrade intent and follow-up match.
3. Deep-review mobile UI/UX and benchmark high-converting, youth-appropriate
   onboarding without manipulative dark patterns; include Framer examples as
   visual references, not templates to copy.
4. Revalidate competitor prices and free limits immediately before checkout
   implementation because offers change over time.
5. Implement account, entitlements, usage meter and Stripe test-mode checkout.
6. Run small channel and offer experiments, then retain only those with healthy
   acquisition cost, activation, retention and contribution margin.
