# Replay Method product demo benchmark — 2026-08-29

## Purpose

This benchmark is a design and product gate for the public Replay Method sample report. It does not authorize detector output, accuracy claims, expert claims or fabricated gameplay evidence.

The target is not to copy another brand. The target is to match the interaction discipline of the strongest AI product demos while remaining native to Replay Method: ten comparable Rocket League replays, a visible recurring decision, attached evidence and one supported next-queue rule.

ElevenLabs is the anchor. Thirty adjacent products were reviewed for product demonstration, input-to-output clarity, hierarchy, motion, trust and conversion structure.

## Binding demo rules

1. The value must be understandable before any animation runs.
2. One bounded product surface must contain the replay moment, result, evidence and action.
3. Five samples are navigation, not five competing campaigns.
4. The selected sample and selected replay moment must be unambiguous.
5. The visitor must reach every sample by click, keyboard and mobile swipe.
6. No autoplay is required to understand the result.
7. Motion may clarify state change; it may not be decorative noise.
8. The input-to-output model must be explicit: ten replays to one supported focus.
9. Evidence language must stay scoped to the illustrative sample.
10. The next action must be one concrete queue rule, not a generic coaching paragraph.
11. The CTA must follow the demonstrated payoff.
12. “Illustrative” must be present but visually secondary.
13. No fake accuracy, rank precision, expert validation or “often detected” claim.
14. No public detector result is implied while public detector gates are closed.
15. No cartoon pitch, fake car choreography or invented gameplay clip.
16. A real clip may be connected only when an authorized source asset exists.
17. Typography, radius, line weight, spacing and color must use the shared site system.
18. Desktop and mobile must preserve the same information order.
19. The output must remain visible without hover.
20. Reduced-motion mode must retain the complete meaning.

## Review model

The release gate contains 30 named aesthetic passes and 30 named technical passes. A pass is a distinct criterion with recorded status; repeated glances without a criterion do not count. The audit covers the demo at desktop and mobile sizes and the surrounding page so the sample report cannot pass in isolation while breaking the funnel.

Real gameplay footage remains a separate asset dependency. Until an owner-authorized clip exists, the public demo uses a clearly bounded replay-media adapter and does not fabricate footage.
