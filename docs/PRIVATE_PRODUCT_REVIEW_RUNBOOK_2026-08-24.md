# Private commercial and UX product reviews

These reviews are product evidence, not Rocket League detector validation. They
use separate accounts, tables, forms and private object prefixes and never
write to `rl_review_labels`.

## Secure access

1. Send the reviewer `https://replaymethod.xyz/product-review`.
2. The reviewer signs in once with their own ChatGPT identity. The first visit
   records a pending request and exposes no test form or private evidence.
3. The owner opens `https://replaymethod.xyz/admin` and finds **Separate product
   reviews**.
4. Assign Nikki **Commercial** for 24 August 2026. Assign Marcel **UX / funnel**
   for 25 August 2026. Approve each person only after matching the displayed
   identity to the intended reviewer.
5. The reviewer reloads the same `/product-review` link. Revocation takes effect
   on the next request.

Do not share credentials, reviewer evidence URLs or RL-review links. An owner
must not assign either product reviewer as a qualified RL detector reviewer
unless that person separately satisfies the complete RL qualification process.

## Nikki — commercial checklist

The structured form covers:

- purchase intent;
- conversion;
- free → paid logic;
- pricing;
- retention;
- LTV;
- acquisition;
- AI-business defensibility;
- commercial risks.

Run the real homepage-to-free path as a first-time buyer. Score every dimension
from 1–5 and record the concrete observation behind the score. The final
recommendation must say ship, change or stop and explain why.

## Marcel — UX / funnel checklist

The structured form covers:

- comprehension;
- number of decisions and clicks;
- feedback speed;
- red thread from entry to next action;
- friction;
- app feel;
- high-octane simplicity.

Run the same real journey on the device and viewport being assessed. Session
notes must identify the path and test context instead of relying on memory.

## Reproducible issue standard

Every recorded issue must contain:

- the concrete problem;
- exact steps or location;
- severity;
- a suggested improvement;
- a screenshot or short screen recording when visual evidence makes the issue
  reproducible.

PNG, JPG, WebP, MP4, WebM and MOV are accepted. Each file is limited to 8 MB and
the combined request to 12 MB. Files are stored under
`product-review-private/` in private R2. Owner downloads require authenticated
admin access and use `private, no-store` plus `nosniff` responses.

Drafts autosave on the current device and can also be saved privately to resume
on another device. Final submission is explicit and irreversible. The owner
dashboard shows lane, state, evidence count and recommendation; its protected
evidence manifest provides the individual download paths.

The owner can revoke access immediately or use **Delete data** to remove the
reviewer identity, drafts, final submission and every linked private evidence
object. Deletion requires a separate destructive-action confirmation and never
touches RL reviewer identities or labels.
