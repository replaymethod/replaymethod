# Replay Method public pricing contract

Last updated: 2026-08-19.

This document contains only the product facts needed to build and present Replay
Method pricing truthfully. Competitor research, unit economics, CAC tolerance and
commercial sensitivity analysis are private.

The earlier $9/month Founding 100 proposal and the later four-duration
$89/$49/$27/$12 ladder are superseded. They are preserved in private decision
history, not offered as beta pricing.

## Selected beta offers

| Offer | Customer charge | Included use | Renewal |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| First diagnosis | $0; no card | One successfully completed, full-quality evidence-backed analysis per verified player during beta | None |
| 3-month cycle — recommended | $27 USD every three months; $9/month effective | Four successfully completed analyses in each 30-day entitlement window | $27 every three months until canceled |
| Monthly | $12 USD per month | Four successfully completed analyses in each 30-day entitlement window | $12 monthly until canceled |

The three-month plan is recommended because an improvement loop needs multiple
matches and the customer saves exactly $9 (25%) versus three current monthly
payments. Public UI must show “$27 charged every three months” at least as
prominently as the $9/month equivalent.

No six- or twelve-month beta plan is offered. An annual plan remains a future
experiment that requires meaningful observed retention, use, refund and support
data.

## What payment changes

Free and paid reports use the same detector quality, confidence thresholds,
abstention rules and evidence requirements. Payment does not buy more flattering
or more certain findings.

The free diagnosis includes:

- one full supported report;
- one primary evidence-backed focus;
- evidence moments, a next-queue rule and a focused plan;
- private access to the completed report;
- no card and no automatic charge.

Paid access adds:

- four completed analyses every 30 days;
- cross-match pattern memory;
- active-focus follow-up;
- longitudinal progress verification;
- private report history.

Unused analyses do not roll over. A blocked input, insufficient-evidence result
or technical failure does not consume an allowance; only a successfully
delivered report does.

## Renewal and cancellation

- Monthly access renews at $12 each month until canceled.
- The three-month cycle renews at $27 every three months until canceled.
- Cancellation is self-service through Stripe Customer Portal.
- Cancellation stops the next renewal and preserves paid access through the
  current paid period.
- Completed reports remain readable after cancellation.
- Refund rights, seller identity, launch markets, taxes and localized currencies
  must reflect the actual production setup and applicable review; do not invent
  them in product copy.

## Public presentation rules

Recommended order:

1. free first diagnosis;
2. recommended three-month cycle;
3. flexible monthly plan.

The recommendation is a transparent label, not a hidden default. Each plan must
show:

- total amount charged;
- billing and renewal period;
- effective monthly price when used;
- exact analysis allowance;
- non-rollover behavior;
- cancellation behavior;
- that completed history remains readable;
- no rank guarantee.

Do not use fake scarcity, countdowns, crossed-out prices, false savings, hidden
renewal, difficult cancellation, disguised charges, consumable token currency or
age-targeted pressure. Visitors under 18 should be asked to involve a parent or
guardian before purchasing.

## Upgrade moments

Do not request a card before the first useful result. Offer paid continuation:

1. after the player has viewed evidence and the primary focus;
2. when the player chooses to track that focus across later matches;
3. when the player submits another replay after the free diagnosis;
4. when history is needed to distinguish recurrence from a one-off.

The primary message remains:

> Keep the coach watching long enough to prove whether the fix worked.

## Measurement before changing the offer

Measure successful free completion, report usefulness, free-to-paid conversion,
plan mix, analysis use, churn, three-month renewal, refunds/disputes, support
confusion and cost per completed report. Do not add a longer commitment merely
to disguise weak retention, and do not weaken the free detector to force
conversion.
